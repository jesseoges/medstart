About MedStart

LEARN • INSPIRE • HEAL

Our purpose

MedStart helps aspiring healthcare professionals explore medical specialties, practise structured clinical reasoning, and learn about training routes. It is an educational project, not a clinical service.

Our founder

MedStart was created by Jesse Ogedengbe, a student interested in medicine, medical research and helping young people explore healthcare careers. The project aims to make medical learning and specialty exploration more accessible, with a focus on Nigerian training pathways.

MedStart is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with any medical school or professional regulator.
