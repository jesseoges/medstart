How To Become a Medical Specialist in Nigeria

Becoming a medical specialist in Nigeria is a long rewarding journey that requires dedication, discipline, and year of a training. Every specialist, whether a surgeon, paediatrician, cardiologist, or neurologist, follows a similar part before choosing a specific field.

STEP 1: Complete secondary school

Obtain at least five credits in WAEC or NECO, including English language, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and physics. also at least 260 and above in JAMB.

STEP 2: Earn an MBBS or MBChB Degree

If u meet the admission requirement of an accredited university offering medicine and you gain admission, study medicine for approximately 6years and gain your degree

STEP 3 : Complete Housemanship

After graduation, complete a one-year internship (housemanship) in an accredited hospital under the supervision of experienced doctors.

STEP 4: Complete NYSC

NYSC is a compulsory one-year programme for most Nigerian graduate. Medical doctors complete it after housemanship, gaining practical experience before specialist training.

STEP 5: Choose a Specialty

Select a specialty such as General Surgery, paediatrics,  internal medicine obstetrics and gynaecology, orthopaedic surgery, cardiology, neurology, or another recongnised field .

STEP 6: Pass the Primary Examination

sit for and pass the primary examination conducted by the appropriate postgraduate medical college(eg WACS). Thus examination tests your knowledge of basic medical sciences relevant to your chosen specialty.

STEP 7: Begin Residency Training

Enter a residency programme in your chosen specialty, where u receive several years of training in accredited hospitals.
