About Medicine

What Is Medicine?
Medicine is the science and practice dedicated to maintaining health, preventing disease, diagnosing medical conditions, and providing effective treatment. It combines scientific knowledge, clinical expertise, and compassionate care to improve the lives of individuals and communities.
Why Medicine Matter

Medicine plays a vital role in society by:
- Preventing diseases through education and vaccination.
- Diagnosing illnesses using modern medical techniques
- Treating injuries and diseases with evidence-based care
- Promoting healthier lives and improving life expectancy

Major Medical Specialties

- General practitioner (GP): they provide primary healthcare and treat common illnesses.
- Surgeon: performs operations to treat diseases and injuries.
- Paediatrician: specializes in healthcare for children.
- Cardiologist: treats diseases of the heart and blood vessels.
- Neurologist: diagnoses and treats disorders of the brain and nervous system.
- Obstetrician & gynaecologist (OB-GYN): provide healthcare for women, including pregnancy and childbirth.

Our Mission

At Medstart, our mission is to make knowledge clear, accessible, and engaging for everyone by introducing the fundamentals of medicine in an informative and inspiring way.
