Measles Cases Rise to the Highest Level in More than Three Decade

Health officials in the United States have reported the climb in the amount of measles infection to their highest level in over 30 decades. Thousands of people have been affected, with many of the cases occurring in areas where vaccination rates have fallen.

Measles is an extremely contagious viral illness that spreads through tiny droplet released when an infected person coughs or sneezes. Early signs include fever, a persistent cough, a runny nose, red eyes, and a rash that gradually spreads across the body. In some people, particularly young children and those with weakened immune systems, the disease can lead to serious complication such as pneumonia, brain inflammation, and in rare cases, death.

Medical experts stress that the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine remains the most effective form of protection. They encourage parents and caregivers to ensure that children receive the recommended doses to help prevent further outbreak.
