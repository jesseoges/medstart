Can Hepatitis B Be Cured? Here's What Science Says

Millions of people are living with hepatitis B without even knowing it. Because the infection often has no early symptoms, it can silently damage the liver for years.

Hepatitis B is a viral infection that attacks the liver and can lead to serious  complications such as cirrhosis and liver cancer if left untreated. It spreads through infected blood and certain body fluids, but not through hugging, sharing food, or shaking hands

So, can it be cured?

At present, there is no complete cure for chronic hepatitis B. However, modern antiviral medicines can control the virus, prevent liver damage, and help people live long, healthy lives.

Scientists are also making exciting progress. In 2026, clinical trials of an experimental drug called bepirovirsen showed that some patients achieved afunctional cure, where the virus become inactive and remained undetectable after treatment.

The best protection is vaccination, early testing, and regular medical care. While a permanent cure is still being researched, the future of hepatitis B treatment has never looked more promising.
