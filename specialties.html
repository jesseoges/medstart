Explore what each field does, the kinds of cases doctors commonly see, a typical route into the specialty in Nigeria, and how earnings generally work.

About earnings: Nigerian public-sector specialist pay is tied to the CONMESS grade and employer, not a unique fixed salary for every specialty. The National Salaries, Incomes and Wages Commission publishes the official salary structures. The 2024 CONMESS implementation gives an entry consultant basic figure of about ₦829,386/month; allowances and private-sector arrangements can change actual earnings. Always check the latest official circular before relying on a figure.

Training information is a simplified educational overview. NPMCN, WACS/WACP and MDCN requirements, accredited centres, curricula and recognition rules can change. Specialist practice in Nigeria requires the appropriate MDCN specialist registration. Image sources and licensing notes are documented in SPECIALTY_PAGE_SOURCES.txt in the MedStart folder.
