MedStart EmailJS setup

The sign-in page now loads EmailJS correctly and never sends/stores the user's password.

To enable sign-in notification emails, the site owner must configure:
EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY
EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID
EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID

The EmailJS template should send the notification to jesseoges@gmail.com and use only:
- user_email
- sign_in_time
- site
Do not include the password.

Until those values are configured, sign-in still works as a local prototype and displays that email notification is not configured.

For real production accounts across devices, replace this localStorage prototype with a proper authentication backend such as Supabase or Firebase.
